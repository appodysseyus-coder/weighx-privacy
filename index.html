WeighX Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026
App: WeighX for Android (com.appodyssey.weigh), including WeighX Pro
Developer: App Odyssey
Contact: app.odyssey.us@gmail.com

WeighX is a weight log that shows a smoothed trend line under your readings. This policy explains what the app does and doesn't do with your information.

The short version

  • WeighX has no internet permission. It can't send anything over the network.
  • Your readings, goal and settings are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • WeighX has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking code, and App Odyssey runs no server for it.
  • We don't sell or share your personal information.

Information we collect

None. WeighX can't connect to the internet, so your weight, body fat, notes, goal, settings and usage never reach App Odyssey.

Information stored only on your device

  • Your readings: date, time, weight, optional body fat percentage and optional note.
  • Your goal and settings: goal weight, height (used to show BMI as a number), units, trend method, reminder time and days, theme and similar choices.
  • Widget snapshot: the trend weight and recent change shown on the home screen widget.

You can delete any reading or use Delete all data in Settings, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app to remove all of it. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the app's data in your own device backup, which Google handles under your Google account settings. We can't see those backups.

Files you import, export or share

When you import a CSV or backup file, you pick it with Android's file picker and the app reads it on your phone. When you export a CSV or backup file, the app saves it where you choose or hands it to the app you pick (for example email or a files app). What happens next is up to you and that app.

Reminders and widget

The optional daily reminder is scheduled on your phone. On Android 13 and later the app asks for notification permission when you turn the reminder on. WeighX re-schedules the reminder after your phone restarts. The widget reads data from the app on your phone.

Health information

Your weight and body fat readings are health-related information. They stay on your phone and are never sent to App Odyssey or anyone else by the app. WeighX does not connect to Health Connect, Google Fit or any other health service. WeighX is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.

Purchases

WeighX Pro will be sold through Google Play. If you buy it, Google Play handles the payment and we don't receive your payment details; the app only learns whether Pro is active on your Google account. During testing, Pro features are unlocked and nothing is sold. When purchases are added, the app will need a connection to Google Play for that check, and this policy will be updated first.

Data sale and sharing

We don't sell, rent or share your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Third-party code

The app is built with open-source libraries (Capacitor and Android Jetpack). It includes no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code. Google Play may collect information when you download or update the app, under Google's own privacy policy.

Children

WeighX is intended for adults. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Security

Data stored on your phone is protected by Android's app storage protections. Exported files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere you trust.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the updated version at this address and change the effective date above.
